# MVP roadmap

Starting point: the station study (building, three tracks, footbridge, waiting room, hall, closed cafeteria, valley, clocks, amber boards). No trains or story yet.

## Station
- [ ] Clocks: fixed night base time that slips while unwatched, instead of the device's real time
- [ ] Summer night sky: Summer Triangle overhead, Plough low in the north-west, faint Milky Way, northern glow
- [ ] Valley opening to the north-east so the glow and sunrise are in view
- [ ] Placeholder announcement (original chime, French), loud on the platform, muffled indoors, faint on the bridge

## Trains and paths
- [ ] Through train on track 2: announced, passes without stopping
- [ ] Passenger train on track 1: announced, stops; boarding leads to the morning ending
- [ ] Open train at the far platform: never announced, arrives silently, blocked from view by the passenger train
- [ ] Waiting on the open train: stepping off again does not count
- [ ] The stranger arrives from the riverside edge, greets, sits; train moves; toast; sunrise
- [ ] The glimpse from the passenger train
- [ ] Exits: street door; platform end into the dark; walking into the through train's light; footbridge railing (test carefully)

## Waking
- [ ] One bedroom for every ending; only light and time differ
- [ ] Dark with a ticking clock (exit and reckless) · morning (passenger) · first light (open train)

## Publishing
- [ ] GitHub Pages
- [ ] Custom domain (CNAME) once chosen
- [ ] GoatCounter events wired through `src/stats.js`
